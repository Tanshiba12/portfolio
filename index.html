About Me & My Journey

Dynamic, creative, and results-driven professional with experience in digital communications, content creation, social media management, creative design, and website development.

Skilled in coordinating teams, managing projects, and leading high-profile events with a strong focus on effective communication and consistent brand delivery. Known for combining technical expertise, creativity, and leadership to drive engagement, streamline operations, and contribute to organizational growth.

Alongside an academic background in Computer Science (Bachelor of Computer Science in Software Engineering and Master of Science in Informatics at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia), my expertise spans multiple domains including digital marketing, content strategy, creative design, web development, and operations management. Throughout my career, I have successfully directed international events, built responsive websites, and driven impactful digital outcomes.

My Story

With an aim to do something independently besides my studies, I started my journey as an entrepreneur in February 2022. Since I was a full-time degree student back then, I founded Embrois, an online-based skincare business that I operated through September 2023. I imported authentic skincare products from Korea, planned business strategies, managed sales, and handled creative design, content creation, and social media marketing. This entrepreneurial foundation developed my commercial and digital instincts, enabling me to explore diverse roles across marketing, design, and event leadership.

Being an enthusiastic person, to align my creative design skills, I joined the multimedia and publicity team at a Research Center (Elite Global Excellence). There I was in charge of designing visually engaging posters and multimedia content, handling publicity materials, and assisting in promotional strategies for workshops and seminars. In October 2023, I worked as a student registration volunteer in the International Student Orientation Program (ISOP) at UTM International, managing critical situations and strengthening my teamwork skills.

Shortly after, in November 2023, I served as part of the protocol and hospitality crew at a major global event, Asia-Pacific Climate Week (APCW) 2023, organized by Persada Johor and held for the first time in Malaysia. In this event, I acted as a primary communicator, maintained event protocols, and facilitated smooth interactions with foreign delegates, VIPs, and foreign Ministers. This experience built my confidence in leading high-profile events with a strong focus on effective communication and team coordination.

In early 2024 (February – April 2024), I took on a role as a customer service cashier at the JDT Football Stadium in Johor Bahru. In this job, I managed financial transactions accurately, handled seasonal subscription memberships, and resolved customer queries to enhance the fan experience.

In October 2024, I joined BTA GlobalX TM as Creative & Digital Communications Executive with an ambition to combine my technological knowledge and versatile creative skills. Leading the company's digital and creative strategy, I managed social media, content creation, creative design, administrative operations, cross-functional team coordination, and website development. In 2025, one of the biggest highlights was organizing the company's global event as Program Director and EMCEE—a full-day Anniversary and BTA Globalx Excellence Awards event attended by delegates from over 30 countries. I led the program from start to finish—managing flow, coordinating teams, creative design, marketing, and developing corporate and event websites.

In parallel, I completed my internship in my Faculty's Quality and Strategy unit, developing an AI-powered Chatbot for FC's Internal Quality Assurance (IQA). Drawing upon my Final Year Project ("Intent Classification of Homestay Booking in Malaysia using Machine Learning models"), I applied natural language processing and model training to architect the chatbot system, embed intelligence, and optimize responses effectively. This synergy between academic research and real-world engineering cemented my technical confidence.

Every experience taught me something new, whether it was building confidence, sharpening my creativity, or orchestrating complex operations. Moving through these diverse domains has shaped me into an adaptable, responsible, and forward-thinking professional ready for high-impact challenges.

AI-Powered Industrial Q&A Chatbot

Developed during industrial training at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM), Faculty of Computing. An intelligent chatbot system that extracts, embeds, and retrieves contextual information from uploaded PDF documents using AI and NLP techniques.

Key Features:
  • Natural language PDF querying with semantic search
  • Role-based access (Visitor, Admin, System Admin)
  • Vector embeddings with all-MiniLM model
  • Real-time chat with context-aware responses
  • Document manager with Google Drive integration
  • Activity monitoring and user management dashboard
Tech Stack:
React.js Node.js Express.js MongoDB Ollama OpenRouter API Tailwind CSS Framer Motion pdf-parse Vector Embeddings
Achievements:

Successfully completed as part of industrial training with full-stack implementation, AI integration, and deployment-ready code. The system handles PDF parsing, semantic chunking, cosine similarity search, and LLM-powered responses with high accuracy and performance.
